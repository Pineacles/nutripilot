# UX pass, 25 Oct 2026

The owner found Statistics, Body, Settings and Scanner cluttered and hard to read after the
colour pass; Today, Weekly and Foods read well and are the reference. The visual world
(DESIGN.md) stays. This pass is about structure, density and navigation. It was analysed with
the UI/UX Pro Max skill (its 119 UX guidelines, rule ids in brackets) against phone screenshots
at 390 px.

## What makes the good pages good

Today, Weekly and Foods each answer one question at the top, then show a short list of ruled
rows a thumb can scan. Detail is one tap away (sources dialog, expand a meal), never laid out
in full.

## Findings

### Navigation
- Seven bottom tabs; the guideline is five at most (bottom-nav-limit). Scanner duplicates the
  barcode button inside Log food, and Settings is secondary navigation (nav-hierarchy).

### Statistics: about 8,000 px on a phone, 16 full charts
- Everything is shown at full detail at once (progressive-disclosure, content-priority,
  data-density). Each chart stacks four layers: daily dots, a 7 day line, a target band and
  alert dots.
- Repeats other pages: Weight and Body composition (Body page), supplement adherence (Today).
  Macro split and Macro trends overlap. The hero row says the same thing three ways
  (90/90 100%, 90 days streak, 100% supplements).
- Seven period chips plus a custom range.
- Vitamins list shows nine rows, most "Enough"; values carry a decimal (446.1).

### Body: about 4,200 px
- The weekly rate appears three times (hero line, cell grid, "Weight in this period" table),
  the total change twice. A six cell grid under the hero competes with it (visual-hierarchy).
- Period chips wrap to two rows.
- Goal progress, the main question after "what do I weigh", sits below the measurement list.
- The measurement list with edit and delete on every row is admin, shown in full.
- Rate of change is an analyst chart.
- No colour: body metrics have no plates.

### Settings: about 5,800 px, one page
- Nine sections on one page (progressive-disclosure); the same nutrients are listed twice
  (tracked switches, then targets); two separate Save buttons while switches save at once
  (submit-feedback, form-autosave inconsistent); Regenerate API key in alert sits mid page
  (destructive-nav-separation).

### Scanner
- A whole tab for something Log food already does; an empty "Result" section on arrival.

## Decisions (owner, 25 Oct 2026)
1. Five tabs: Today, Weekly, Foods, Stats, Body. Settings is a gear at the top right of every
   page (phones) and stays in the sidebar on desktop. Scanner is reached from Log food and a
   Scan button on Foods; /scanner keeps working as a route.
2. Settings becomes an index of sections, each a sub page; each nutrient row holds its switch
   and its target together; everything saves automatically with a short "Saved" tick.
3. Body gets colour: muscle #10aa91, body fat #a1790c (validated pair on g0); weight stays ink.

## The pattern every page follows
1. One question answered at the top (one display figure or none).
2. A scorecard: ruled rows, each with name, the answer in words, a small visual (sparkline or
   bar) and a status word. Tap a row to open its full chart or detail in place.
3. Detail and admin behind a tap, not on the page.
4. Each fact appears once.
